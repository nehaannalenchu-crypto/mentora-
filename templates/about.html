THE IDEA

About the Project

This online tutoring platform is being developed as part of my second-year Java Programming project. The main objective is to create an accessible, interactive, and user-friendly platform that connects students with suitable tutors based on their academic needs.

The platform aims to simplify the process of finding tutors, encourage personalized learning, and provide a convenient digital environment for both students and educators.

My Role in the Project

As the developer of this project, I am involved in planning, designing, and implementing the online tutoring platform.

My responsibilities include designing the website's user interface, developing its features, implementing Java programming concepts, and working on the functionality that allows students and tutors to register and connect.

How Mentora recommends tutors ⌄

Closest fits first

Profiles are ranked by subject, schedule, budget, and learning preferences. Imperfect matches stay visible with the differences explained.

Learning from feedback

The prototype uses weighted scoring until a Logistic Regression model is trained with suitable feedback. Fictional demo tutors are excluded from training feedback.

A guide to choosing

A fit score helps you compare preferences. It does not guarantee tutoring success or learning improvement.
